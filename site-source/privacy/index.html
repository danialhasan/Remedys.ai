Privacy · Updated October 30, 2026

Your information.
Handled with care.

What this website collects, why we use it, and how to reach us about it.

What you provide

When you send an inquiry, we collect your name, email, company or organization, optional service interest, and the description you submit. Please do not include passwords, sensitive personal records, or confidential client information.

How we use it

We use inquiry details to understand your request, respond, and discuss potential work. We do not sell your inquiry information or add you to a marketing list through this form.

Services that process information

Netlify hosts the website and runs the inquiry endpoint. Inquiry details are stored in Supabase and may be sent to our team through Resend for follow-up. When you use the booking link, Google processes the details you enter under its own privacy terms. Your email provider processes messages you send by email.

Technical information

Our hosting provider may process request information such as IP address, browser details, and access logs to deliver and protect the site. The inquiry endpoint uses rate limiting to reduce abuse. This version of the website does not load third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or remote fonts, and does not set analytics cookies or store inquiry drafts in your browser.

Retention and your choices

We keep inquiry information as needed to respond, discuss work, and maintain relevant business records. To ask about information you submitted, request a correction, or request deletion, contact hello@remedys.ai. We will review the request and any applicable retention obligations.

Contact

Questions about this website or your information can be sent to hello@remedys.ai.
