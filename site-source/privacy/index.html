Privacy · Updated October 30, 2026

How we use
your information.

What this website collects, why we use it, and how to reach us about it.

What the form collects

When you send the form, we collect your name, email address, organization and description of the work. You can also tell us which service interests you. Please do not include passwords, sensitive personal records, or confidential client information.

How we use it

We use these details to understand your question, reply and discuss possible work. We do not sell your inquiry information or add you to a marketing list through this form.

Who handles your information?

Netlify hosts this website and runs the form. We store your form answers in Supabase and may send them to our team by email through Resend. When you use the booking link, Google processes the details you enter under its own privacy terms. Your email provider processes messages you send by email.

Technical information

Our hosting provider may process request information such as IP address, browser details, and access logs to deliver and protect the site. The form limits how often someone can send a request to help prevent abuse. This version of the website does not load third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or remote fonts, and does not set analytics cookies or store inquiry drafts in your browser.

How long do we keep your information?

We keep inquiry information as needed to respond, discuss work, and maintain relevant business records. To ask about information you submitted, request a correction, or request deletion, contact hello@remedys.ai. We will review your request and check which records we have to keep.

Contact

Questions about this website or your information can be sent to hello@remedys.ai.
