A closer look

Learn with the work
in front of you.

Access to a tool does not tell people when to use it or how to check the answer. We build training around relevant tasks so your team can practice both the workflow and the judgment it needs.

  1. 01

    Choose relevant tasks

    Understand the tools and responsibilities. Use examples that fit the work and the information people can safely use.

  2. 02

    Practice and review

    Work through prompts and workflows. Compare outputs, examine limits, and practice checking results.

  3. 03

    Keep the guidance useful

    Document agreed practices and examples. Scope follow-up support around the questions the team needs to resolve.

Made to fit the engagement.

Deliverables, timing, cost, and ongoing support are agreed before work starts.
