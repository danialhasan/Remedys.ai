AI training

Learn to use AI
on your own work.

We teach your team using tasks they recognize. They try the tool, find its mistakes and practice checking an answer before they use it.

How it works
Example · A customer reply

“Can you send me a price for next month?”

Read
The customer hasn’t said what they want, how many they need or the exact date.
Draft
“What would you like to order, how many do you need, and for which date?”
Check
Read the draft before sending it. Your team still sets the price and confirms the date.

What we can help you with

  • Exercises from your team's work
  • Time to practice and ask questions
  • Examples to use afterward

What happens
in a workshop?

Tell us what people need help doing. A new user might practice checking an email draft. A group of researchers might check a plan for an experiment.

What your team can practiceWorkshops and upcoming dates

  1. 01

    Pick a task

    Choose something the team does often and an example they can safely share.

  2. 02

    Try it together

    Write a request for the tool, look at the answer and check it against what you know.

  3. 03

    Take it back to work

    Keep examples and instructions from the session. Agree on any help the team will need afterward.

Before we start, we agree on what you’ll get, what it costs, how long it will take and who will help afterward.
