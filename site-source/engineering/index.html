What would the first
version let you do?

Pick one task and the people who will use the tool. That gives the team something small enough to try and useful enough to judge.

Get help running the tool after launch

  1. 01

    Agree on the job

    Choose what the tool must do, what it can leave out and which apps it needs to work with.

  2. 02

    Try a working version

    Your team tries it on examples from the job. We use their feedback to change the parts that need work.

  3. 03

    Get ready to use it

    Test who can see the information and what happens when the tool fails. Agree on instructions and who will maintain it.

Before we start, we agree on what you’ll get, what it costs, how long it will take and who will help afterward.
