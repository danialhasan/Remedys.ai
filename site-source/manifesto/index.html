Our principles

How we scope,
build, and hand over.

A few beliefs that guide how we think, build, and work with people.

01

The work comes first.

A new tool is a means to an end. We begin with the people, the process, and the outcome that needs to change.

02

Compare AI with simpler options.

Review the value, effort, data needs, and risks. An existing tool or integration may be a better fit.

03

Build with the people who use it.

Real workflows have exceptions. The people closest to the work help us find them and test the solution.

04

Make judgment part of the system.

Decide what needs review, who owns a decision, and what happens when something goes wrong.

05

Plan how the team will use it.

Documentation, training, and clear ownership help the work continue beyond the project.
