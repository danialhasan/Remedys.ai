Our principles

How we decide
what to build.

These are the questions we ask before building a tool and before leaving it with your team.

01

What job needs to change?

We ask the person who does the job to show us the last time they did it.

02

Would a simpler fix work?

We compare AI with changing a step or using a tool you already own. We look at cost, time and what could go wrong.

03

Who will use it?

Your team tries a working version. They can spot a missing step or a case we overlooked.

04

Who checks the result?

We agree on what a person needs to check and who decides what happens if the tool gets it wrong.

05

Who looks after the tool?

Your team gets instructions and practice using the tool. We agree on who to contact when it needs a fix.
