Shafan Khan and Danial Hasan led a Codex workshop for computer science faculty and graduate students at East Carolina University.

June 2, 2026

One exercise in the workshop materials starts with a research paper. Researchers use Codex, an AI coding tool, to plan an experiment. They choose what to measure and check which ideas in the plan still need evidence.
