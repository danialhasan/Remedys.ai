A closer look

A decision you
can explain.

A vendor proposal, a long list of ideas, or a process that has become difficult to manage. We start with the work and the people doing it, then compare AI with simpler options.

  1. 01

    Understand the work

    Walk through the tasks, tools, handoffs, and constraints with the people closest to the process.

  2. 02

    Compare the options

    Look at potential value, effort, data needs, and risk. An existing tool or a clearer process may be enough.

  3. 03

    Define the next step

    Set out what to try, why it matters, and which assumptions need testing before a larger commitment.
