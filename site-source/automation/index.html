A closer look

The gaps between tools
are someone’s workload.

An email becomes a spreadsheet entry. Someone checks another system, finds the owner, then remembers to follow up. We map those handoffs before choosing what to automate.

  1. 01

    Map the handoffs

    Identify the trigger, inputs, decisions, and systems. Agree who handles missing information and unusual cases.

  2. 02

    Connect the steps

    Build around the agreed process. Use AI where interpretation helps, with review before consequential actions.

  3. 03

    Plan for exceptions

    Test incomplete inputs, unavailable tools, and recovery. Agree how the workflow will be monitored and maintained.

Made to fit the engagement.

Deliverables, timing, cost, and ongoing support are agreed before work starts.
