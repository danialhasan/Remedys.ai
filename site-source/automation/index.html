Workflow automation

Stop entering the same
details twice.

We connect the tools you already use. A customer request can go from your inbox to the right person without someone copying it by hand.

How it works
Example · A customer email

Who will answer this request?

Receive
A customer sends an email. The request appears on the team’s work list.
Prepare
A tool gathers the details and drafts a reply.
Check
A team member checks the details and decides what to send.

What we can help you with

  • A map of the steps
  • Tools that share the right details
  • A plan for errors

What happens after
the email arrives?

Someone may copy the email into a spreadsheet, find the right person and remind them to follow up. We start by looking for steps your tools could handle.

Get help running the tool after launch

  1. 01

    Follow a request

    Trace one request from its first message to the last reply. Find out who handles it if a detail is missing.

  2. 02

    Connect the tools

    Build and test the steps together. Your team decides what needs a person’s approval.

  3. 03

    Try a problem case

    Test what happens when a detail is missing or a tool goes offline. Agree on who will fix it.

Before we start, we agree on what you’ll get, what it costs, how long it will take and who will help afterward.
