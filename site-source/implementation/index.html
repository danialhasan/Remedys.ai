01

Choose

Choose one job.

Show us the last time you did it. We follow the steps and ask where people wait, repeat work or fix mistakes.

Before we build

What would make the job easier?

We agree on one change to try and a way to check whether it helped. Sometimes a change to a form is enough.

02

Build

Try it on real examples.

We make a small working version. Your team tries it on an ordinary job and on one where something went wrong.

Before we add more

Can the team trust the result?

We look for missing facts, fix mistakes and agree on what still needs a person to check.

03

Teach

Learn to run the tool.

The people who will use the tool practice with it. We write down the steps and what to do when it makes a mistake.

Before we finish

Who looks after it?

We agree on who has access, who can fix problems and what help you want from us afterward.

After launch

Who keeps the tool running?

You can run it with your own team, or hire Remedys for ongoing support. We can run the tool, fix problems and improve it as your work changes.

Workflow worksheet

Pick one task you do often. Change the example below to describe your own work. You can print it or save a PDF. Your edits stay in this tab.

Which task keeps coming up?

Turning a new client’s email into a project brief, a short note about what they need.

What starts the work?

A new project request arrives by email. The project lead reads it before the first call.

Which step do you repeat?

The lead copies the goals, deadline and open questions into a separate document.

What will you try first?

Use the same blank form for the next five requests. Ask AI to fill it in from the email and list any questions the client hasn’t answered.

Who checks the result?

The project lead checks the draft against the original email before sharing the brief.

How will you decide whether to keep it?

Compare how long these five briefs took with the last five. Write down what the lead had to fix or add.
